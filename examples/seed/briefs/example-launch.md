# Brief: Acme Analytics: Scheduled Reports launch

Requested by: Product marketing
Target URL: /product/scheduled-reports

## Goal

Get analytics team leads to try Scheduled Reports. Primary conversion is a demo booking; secondary is a free trial.

## Audience

Analytics team leads who spend Monday mornings exporting the same dashboards for the same stakeholders.

## Key message

Set a report up once, and Acme Analytics delivers it to the right people, on schedule, with the numbers already checked.

## Sections

1. **Stop the Monday export.** Reports go out on a schedule to email or chat, with no manual copying.
2. **Checked before they're sent.** Each report runs its data checks first and holds itself back if a source failed to refresh.
3. **Everyone sees their own slice.** One report, filtered per recipient, using the permissions they already have.

## Confirmed facts (OK to publish)

- Schedules can run hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly.
- Delivery to email, Slack, and Microsoft Teams.
- Reports are held back automatically when a source table is stale.

## Approved quote

"We used to spend half of Monday on exports. Now the reports are waiting in everyone's inbox when they log in." — Jordan Lee, Head of Analytics, Example Co.

## Questions to answer

- Does it work with our existing dashboards? Yes, any saved dashboard can be scheduled.
- Who can see a scheduled report? Only recipients who already have access to the underlying data.
